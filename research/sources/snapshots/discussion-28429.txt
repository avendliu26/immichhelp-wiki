I am going through some challenges after using an external library. I don't know if it will affect you, but I just learned that external lib aren't part of the dedupe process. I'm getting a bunch of uniqueness constraint issues now because I tried copying a previous library (non-external) and adding it as an external lib to a new instance. It messed everything up. I'm in the middle of debugging it now, but I would say avoid external if you can.
Use SSD paths for thumbs and encoded-video if you can:
https://docs.immich.app/guides/custom-locations

@marr
Sorry but I use mostly only external library without any issues.
And the duplicate tool - like everything else - is working for them.
In fact I don't see any limitation with external libraries at all. I think you are facing "something else".
All my assets are in external libraries on a homeserver and exported as NFS drive. They are served by Immich, but also accessed by other applications (Kodi mainly).
My process is uploading new content directly in Immich, to save and make them immediately available everywhere, so they land in the main user library (not external library).
Then, every quarter or so, I take them out and process them:
do some triage (duplicate, same scene, bad quality pictures, ...)
file renaming to uniform them
aligning metadata (tagging)
reducing the  JPEG Quality
re-encoding video (from fast/low compression H265 encoding of camera/smartphone) to AV1 with high-quality/compression ratio
creating dedicated sub-folders for big events
As final step, once they are properly cleaned-up/organized, the data size went down by 30 to 50% and they are ready for lifetime storage and
I move them to the external library
.
The advantage of external library is that y
ou can do whatever changes directly on the filesystem
, without any risk with Immich database. Of course you have to reprocess the external library after that to update, but this is not corrupting the database.
On the other hand with "upload library", you cannot touch the file from the filesystem/backend, this is
not
supported, this should
never be done
, this will let orphan references in the databse, cause access/error messages.
And that is the reason why you need to be careful with the "moving out of upload library" step. You have to:
make a copy of those asset in the filesystem
do your processing (external to Immich)
in Immich
, select/identify those uploaded assets, and delete them
add the processed items to the external library
That identification step can be easily done, by going to the
Folders
view, then go to your
/data/library/...
content, they will be there. You can CTRL+A / select all, then just _delete.
