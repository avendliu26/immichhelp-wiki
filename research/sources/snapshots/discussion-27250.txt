I have searched the existing feature requests, both open and closed, to make sure this is not a duplicate request.
Yes
The feature
On iOS, the Immich app stops uploading photos as soon as the app is closed or sent to the background. Upload only resumes when the app is reopened manually.
This makes automatic backup unreliable compared to other photo backup apps.
To Reproduce
Steps to reproduce the behavior:
Open the Immich app on iPhone
Enable photo backup
Take several new photos
send it to background
Wait some time
Expected behavior
Photos should continue uploading in the background, or at least resume automatically without requiring the user to reopen the app.
Actual behavior
Uploads stop completely when the app is closed. They only resume when reopening the app.
Environment (please complete the following information):
Device: iPhone (model: [e.g. iPhone 13])
iOS version: [e.g. iOS 17.x]
Immich app version: [e.g. 1.xx.x]
Server version: [e.g. 1.xx.x]
Installation method: [Docker / other]
Additional context
Background App Refresh is enabled
Low Power Mode is disabled
Full photo access is granted
Issue persists even under optimal conditions
This appears to be related to iOS background execution limitations, but other apps (e.g., Google Photos) seem to handle this more reliably.
It would be helpful to clarify whether this is a limitation of iOS or if improvements/workarounds are planned.
