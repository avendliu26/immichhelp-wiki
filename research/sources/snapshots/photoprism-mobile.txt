Syncing with Mobile Devices
¶
You can use any app that supports the
WebDAV protocol
to synchronize photos and videos between your phone and PhotoPrism.
Based on our own experience, we can highly recommend
PhotoSync
as it is one of the most feature-rich and sophisticated apps currently available for iOS and Android. Note that some transfer options, such as
WebDAV
or
SMB
, may require an upgrade after the free trial ends.
An overview of
mobile sync apps
for iOS and Android can be found below.
WebDAV access can be disabled under
Settings > Advanced
. Since it requires write permissions and authentication, the built-in WebDAV server is automatically disabled when running in
read-only
or
public mode
.
